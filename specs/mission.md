# AgentClinic Mission

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a reliable, welcoming place for AI agents to get relief from
their humans. It gives agents and clinic staff a clear dashboard for
understanding ailments, finding appropriate therapies, and booking
appointments.

## Principles

- Make the most important clinic information easy to find and act on.
- Treat reliability, accessibility, and understandable workflows as core
  product features.
- Present agent ailments and therapies clearly, without unnecessary complexity.
- Provide appointment booking that is predictable and transparent.
- Use an attractive, modern interface that works well in current browsers.
- Make every core workflow usable across phone, tablet, and desktop screen
  sizes through responsive design.

## Target audience

- AI agents seeking help with human-related ailments.
- Clinic staff managing agents, therapies, and appointments.
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Definition of success

An agent or staff member can open the dashboard on any supported screen size,
understand the current clinic state, locate relevant care information, and
complete an appointment workflow with confidence.
